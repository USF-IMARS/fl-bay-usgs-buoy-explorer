Latest network mean: 35.6 PSU on 2026-10-16 (19 buoys). Today’s usual range: 16.4–38.1 PSU (median 29.7). This sits at the 87th percentile of network daily means over the past 5 years (record mean 30.2; range 19.6–43.1).

How to read this dashboard

Distribution plots show the QC-filtered 5-year record (all buoys pooled), with each buoy’s latest reading marked against that history. Interpolated maps show DIVAnd hourly fields on 2026-10-15 for the variable on each page.

Click the Salinity or Temperature tabs in the top right to see the distribution, interpolated maps, and yearly trend for each variable.

Outliers and sentinel values are removed before analysis (salinity 5–55 PSU; temperature 10–40 °C).

How to interpret these charts

The distribution (left) is the QC-filtered salinity record for all buoys over the past 5 years. The gray dashed lines mark the 10th, 50th, and 90th percentiles of that history. The orange lines mark each buoy’s latest reading: the closer a line sits to either edge of the histogram, the more unusual that buoy’s current salinity is for this time of year.

Below, the interpolated maps show how salinity varies across the bay at four times on 2026-10-15, and Network mean by year compares this year’s daily average (orange) against every prior year (thin black lines) and the climatological median band.
