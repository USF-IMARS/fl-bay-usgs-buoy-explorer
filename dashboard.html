About this dashboard

This static dashboard combines Leaflet htmlwidgets (Quarto interactivity docs) with DIVAnd variational interpolation and bathymetry-aware land masking from the south-florida-salinity project.

Lower salinity (blue) indicates fresher water; higher values (red) indicate more marine conditions. Practical salinity units (PSU) are parts per thousand.

Interpolation method

Fields use DIVAnd at ~0.01° resolution with a two-scale analysis (0.08° background + 0.035° local anomalies, epsilon2 = 0.01), run separately within each connected sea component. Land is masked with Natural Earth coastlines and NOAA BlueTopo 4 m bathymetry. Four panels show snapshots at 06:00, 12:00, 18:00, and 24:00 US Eastern on 2026-10-15.
